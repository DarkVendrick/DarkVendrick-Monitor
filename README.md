## Experience PC Monitoring Differently

DarkVendrick PC Monitor combines real-time hardware telemetry, automatic gaming session tracking and detailed performance analytics with a unique dark fantasy visual experience.

Monitor your PC performance, analyze your gaming sessions and explore a dynamic fantasy environment that reacts to weather conditions and time of day.

## Features

- **CPU & GPU Monitoring** – Real-time temperatures, loads and hardware statistics
- **FPS & 1% Low** – Track gaming performance and frame-rate consistency
- **Automatic Game Detection** – Record gaming sessions automatically
- **Gaming History** – Analyze previous sessions and performance trends
- **Network Monitoring** – Monitor network activity and connection statistics
- **Fantasy Weather Engine** – Dynamic environments with weather and time-of-day variations
- **Live Dashboard** – Dedicated monitoring interface with secondary-display support
- **BlackBox Diagnostics** – Export diagnostic information for troubleshooting
